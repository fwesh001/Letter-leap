Achievements

Master the Leap

Stack streaks, hunt rare letters, and unlock your path to glory. Every medal and achievement is a step towards becoming a Word Wizard.

Medal Overview

Streak Medals

Earned by playing correct words in a row without mistakes. High streaks grant score bonuses.

Bronze x3 Silver x5 Gold x7 Platinum x10 Diamond x15

Rare Letters

Use letters: q, x, u, z, v, w, y.

Bonus points for special words like "leap" or "letter".

Bronze (1 word) Silver (5 words) Gold (10 words)

Long Words

Play long words to boost your score and medal progress (Length ≥ 12).

Bronze (1 word) Silver (5 words) Gold (10 words)

In-Game Challenges

Double Trouble
Use a word with two consecutive identical letters.
Tip: Examples include "letter", "coffee", "bottle", "supper".
Vowel Master
Play a word with at least 4 vowels.
Look for vowel-heavy words like "equation", "audience", "beautiful".
Consonant Crusher
Play a word with 5 or more consonants.
Try dense words like "strength", "transcript", "construct".
Quick Thinker
Play 15 words in a row without letting the timer drop below 30s.
Keep the pace high! Submit as fast as you can to maintain the bonus.
Rebounder
Play a word that starts with the last letter of the AI's word and is longer.
Check the AI's last word; respond with a longer word starting with its last letter.
Silent but Deadly
Use a word with no traditional vowels (A, E, I, O, U).
Think words like "rhythm", "myth", "crypt", "glyph".
Wildcard
Use a word that contains all 5 vowels.
Examples: "sequoia", "education", "aurelio".
Letter Jumper
Play a word that skips the next alphabetical letter from the previous word's end.
If the previous word ended with "C", a valid jump start might be "E" (skipping "D").
Last Second Hero
Still alive when only 10 seconds remain on your turn.
Survive with exactly or less than 10 seconds left on your turn timer.

Pro Tip: Matches track Max Streak, Long Words, Rare Letters, and total Achievements unlocked. Aim for a balanced profile to dominate the leaderboard!
