Achievements & Medals

Master Letter Leap by stacking streaks, hunting rare letters, playing long words, and unlocking unique challenges. This explains how every medal and achievement works so you can plan your path to glory.

Medals Overview

Streak Medals

Earned by playing correct words in a row without mistakes. Hitting these streaks also grants in-game score bonuses.

  • Bronze 3-streak — Bonus +1
  • Silver 5-streak — Bonus +3
  • Gold 7-streak — Bonus +5
  • Platinum 10-streak — Bonus +7
  • Diamond 15-streak — Bonus +10

Rare Letters Medal

Play words that contain rare letters to earn score bonuses and progress this medal.

Counts letters: q, x, u, z, v, w, y — plus special words: “leap”, “letter”.
  • Bronze Use 1 rare-letter word
  • Silver Use 5 rare-letter words
  • Gold Use 10 rare-letter words

Long Words Medal

Play long words to boost your score and medal progress.

Counts any word with length ≥ 12.
  • Bronze Play 1 long word
  • Silver Play 5 long words
  • Gold Play 10 long words

In‑Game Achievements

🅰🅰
Double Trouble
Use a word that has two consecutive identical letters.
Tip: Examples include “letter”, “coffee”, “bottle”.
🎤
Vowel Master
Play a word with at least 4 vowels.
Look for long or vowel-heavy words like “equation”, “audience”.
🔨
Consonant Crusher
Play a word with 5 or more consonants.
Try dense words like “strength”, “transcript”, “construct”.
🧠💡
Quick Thinker
Play 15 words in a row without letting the timer drop below 30 seconds.
Keep a fast rhythm: submit quickly when it’s your turn to maintain ≥ 30s left.
🔁
Rebounder
Play a word that starts with the last letter of the AI’s word and is longer.
Check the AI’s last word; respond with a longer word starting with its last letter.
🤫
Silent but Deadly
Use a word with no traditional vowels.
Think words like “rhythm”, “myth”, “crypt”, “glyph”.
🃏
Wildcard
Use a word that contains all 5 vowels.
Examples: “sequoia”, “education”, “aurelio”.
🦘
Letter Jumper
Play a word that skips the next alphabetical letter from the previous word’s end.
If the previous word ended with “c”, a valid jump start might be “e” (skips “d”).
⏰
Last Second Hero
Still alive when only 10 seconds remain.
Survive with exactly 10 seconds left on your turn timer.

Note: In matches, your extra stats include Max Streak, Long Words used, Rare Letters used, and total Achievements unlocked.
