📖 How to Play Letter-Leap

👤 Single Player

1. You’ll get a random starting letter.

2. Type a valid word starting with that letter.

3. The AI responds with the last letter — your turn again!

4. +5 seconds for every correct word ⏳

5. Score big, unlock achievements, laugh at the game-over quotes 😂

👥 Multiplayer Mode

1. Create or join a room with your custom username.

2. Room creator sets the starting letter — or let the game choose.

3. Take turns sending words. First letter must match opponent’s last letter.

  • +1 point per word, bonus points for streaks & rare letters 💎
  • -1 point after every 2 incorrect attempts 🚫
  • Run out of time? You lose ⏳
  • Optional: Add an AI bot to spice things up 🤖
  • First to reach the score limit (default 20) wins 🏆

    • Tip: Watch the timer and plan your next word fast!
